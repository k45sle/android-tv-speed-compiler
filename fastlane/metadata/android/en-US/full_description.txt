Android normally compiles an app bit by bit, based on how you use it. Speed Compile asks the TV to compile the apps you choose ahead of time, with Android's speed-profile filter, and does it again whenever one of them updates. It talks to the TV through the TV's own Wireless debugging, so you don't need a computer once it is set up.

Results vary by app and by TV. Some apps open or scroll more smoothly, and some show no difference. Each compiled app takes a bit more storage.

What it does:
• Watches the apps you pick and queues a compile when one of them updates. You can pick several apps at once.
• Gets most apps ready right after an update by loading the usage profile they ship with, then refreshes the compile over the next week as you use them.
• Runs jobs only when the TV is idle, when nothing is playing, or during hours you set, so it doesn't slow down what you are watching.
• Measures how smoothly each app runs while you use it. If an app is still choppy, Smoother mode compiles it fully, and Back to normal mode undoes that.
• Checks once a week, and after a system update, that each app is still compiled.
• Reset puts an app back the way the TV had it.
• Reconnects by itself after the TV restarts, and turns Wireless debugging back on when it needs it (you can turn this off in Settings).
• Has an arcade with Alien Run, Star Arrow and Cosmic Worm, open while a compile runs or any time from Home.
• Keeps a history of each job, with the reason when one fails.
• Comes in light and dark themes, with eight accent colours plus a custom one.

Setup walks you through Developer options and Wireless debugging, then shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone and type the 6-digit pairing code from the TV.

No account, no ads, no analytics. The app connects only to the TV it runs on, and to your phone on the same network while you pair or download diagnostics. The debugging key is made on the TV and stays in the app's private storage.

Needs Android TV or Google TV 11 or later. It has been tested on the Android TV emulator and on one Android 14 TV box, and not yet on Fire TV.
